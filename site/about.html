What it does

Paste anything. Display beautifully.

DemoTab is a single browser tab you load before a screen share or presentation. Paste your content—code, Markdown notes, a screenshot, or a block of text—and it renders cleanly for your audience without the distractions of your usual editor.

📝

Plain text

Clean, readable typography. No line-wrap surprises and no clutter.

#️⃣

Markdown

CommonMark-compatible headings, nested lists, links, tables, code blocks, and more.

🌐

HTML source

Syntax-highlighted source that is displayed cleanly and never executed.

💻

Source code

Local syntax highlighting for more than ten languages with line numbers.

🖼️

Images

Paste or drop an image and display it centered on a neutral background.

⚡

Automatic detection

Paste content and DemoTab determines the most useful display mode.

Privacy

We genuinely cannot see what you paste.

This is enforced by your browser. DemoTab ships with a Content Security Policy that makes application data connections technically impossible.

Browser-enforced isolation

connect-src 'none' tells the browser to refuse every attempt by the application to send data anywhere. Markdown images that refer to remote or relative files are also blocked before they can make a request.

All rendering happens locally with same-origin scripts and styles. There are no servers receiving your content, no logs, and no databases.

How to use it

Three steps.

  1. 1. Open DemoTab before your screen share or presentation.
  2. 2. Share that tab—or your whole screen—with your audience.
  3. 3. Paste or drop whatever you want to show. On mobile, use the paste button or the dedicated paste box. Press Esc or select Clear to reset.
